The Cansat competition is a design-build-fly competition that provides teams
with an opportunity to experience the design life-cycle of an aerospace system.
The Cansat competition is designed to reflect a typical aerospace program on a
small scale and includes all aspects of an aerospace program from the
preliminary design review to post flight review. The mission and its
requirements are designed to reflect various aspects of real world missions
including telemetry, communications, and autonomous operations. Each team is
scored throughout the competition on real-world deliverables such as schedules,
design review presentations, and demonstration flights.
