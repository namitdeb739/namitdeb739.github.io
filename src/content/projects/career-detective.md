Our project analyzes AI and Tech job market trends to help people better
understand career opportunities in Germany compared to other developed
countries. We aim to build an interactive web app or art visualization to
demonstrate AI job availability, salary distributions, entry-level
opportunities, and regional demand across countries. Rather than relying on
fragmented job boards, we will use machine learning to aggregate and analyze
large scale job posting data. This project will help graduates and job seekers
to better navigate the current job market as well as help students to
familiarize with ongoing trends to prepare earlier.
